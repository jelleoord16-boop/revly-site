Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026. The Dutch version of this policy is leading if the two differ.

In short
  • Your cars, maintenance, trips and photos are stored only on your own iPhone.
  • Revly has no account and no server of its own. We can't see your file.
  • For the weather and the map, your location goes to Apple and Google, only when you use those features.
  • We only send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports if you give permission.
  • We never use your data for advertising and we don't sell anything on.

Who is responsible?

Revly is made by Jelle, a car enthusiast from the Netherlands. You can ask privacy questions via support@getrevly.nl.

What is stored on your phone?

Everything you enter in Revly is stored locally on your iPhone. Think of your cars and registrations, maintenance and costs, reminders, odometer readings, fill-ups and charging sessions, passport entries, insurance details, road tax and lease details, your car's purchase price and value if you enter them, photos of your car, receipts and invoices (including PDFs), pages of your service book, and trips.

If your iPhone's iCloud backup is on, Apple includes this data in your own backup. Revly has no access to it.

Trips and location

If you turn on Track trips, Revly uses your location, also in the background, to recognise car trips. The distance, the times and (if you want) the route are stored only on your phone. You confirm yourself whether you were driving before a trip counts.

In Profile > Data & privacy you choose whether routes are kept and for how long (30 days, 90 days, 1 year or always). The app deletes older routes automatically. You can turn trip tracking off at any time.

Business trips (Revly Plus)

If you turn on Keep track of business trips, Revly also keeps the start and end point of every trip, even if you don't keep routes. The app uses them to look up the address through Apple Maps, so it appears in your trip log. For the address, those two points are sent to Apple. Start and end points, addresses, whether a trip is business or private and your description stay on your phone until you delete the trip. If you turn the setting off, Revly no longer keeps new start and end points.

Scanning receipts and invoices (Revly Plus)

When you scan a fuel receipt or garage invoice, or pick a photo, screenshot or PDF, Revly reads the text on your iPhone itself, using Apple's text recognition. Nothing is sent to a server. The camera only asks for permission the first time you scan. You pick photos yourself with the iOS photo picker, so Revly doesn't see the rest of your photo library.

Scanning your service book

When you photograph the pages of your service book, Revly reads the dates, odometer readings and garages on your iPhone itself, also using Apple's text recognition. Nothing is sent to a server. You check what was found before it is saved. The app keeps the photo of a page with the service entry, on your phone. The first service book is free, after that it is part of Revly Plus.

Map with fuel stations, charging points and garages

The map itself is Apple's (Apple Maps). Revly looks up fuel stations, charging points and garages through Google Places. For this, the area you are looking at on the map is sent to Google, and for brand garages your car's brand as a search term (for example "Volvo garage"). When you tap a place, Revly fetches its name, address, rating and opening hours from Google using the ID of that place. Google also receives ordinary technical data, such as your IP address. Your registration number and other data from your file never go to Google. Revly doesn't keep anything from Google on your phone.

If you search for a town or postcode, Apple looks it up. If you choose Google Maps for a route, that app (or the website) opens with the destination. Google's privacy policy applies to that. If Google is briefly unavailable, Revly uses Apple's search instead.

Exporting

When you export your costs or trip log to Excel, Revly creates the file on your phone. You choose in the share sheet where it goes, for example to your email or your employer. Revly doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Usage statistics and crash reports

Revly uses Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring) to see which features are used and to fix bugs. This is off by default. It only turns on if you give permission in the welcome flow or in Profile > Data & privacy.

With permission, the app sends things like: which screens and features you use (for example "maintenance logged" or "trip confirmed"), general device information such as the iPhone model and iOS version, a random app ID and, in case of a crash, technical details about the error. We never send your registration number, routes, notes, receipts, garage names or other content of your file. From the map we only measure how many places were found and a short error code if a search fails, never which places, where you searched or what you searched for.

Advertising measurement is always off. Firebase is a service of Google Ireland Limited. Google may also process data outside the EU, with the safeguards Google applies for this. Statistics are kept for up to 14 months. If you withdraw your permission, the app stops measuring immediately and the statistics on your phone are deleted.

The legal basis for this is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time, without the app working any less well.

Revly Plus

A Revly Plus subscription runs through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. Revly only learns from Apple whether your subscription is active, so the Plus features work. You cancel in your Apple ID settings. The standard Apple terms of use (EULA) apply.

Notifications

Reminders for maintenance, trips, seasonal tips, milestones (such as a round odometer reading or your car's birthday) and the fuel use alert are local notifications. They are scheduled on your phone, and nothing goes through a server. In Profile > Notifications you turn each type on or off.

Your rights

  • Access and take with you: via Profile > Data & privacy > Export my data you get all your data in one file.
  • Delete: via Delete everything on the same screen, or by deleting the app. Because we have no server, your data is then nowhere with us.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off the switches for statistics and crash reports.
  • Questions or a complaint: e-mail us at the address above. If we can't work it out together, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or with the supervisory authority in your own country.

Children

Revly is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If anything changes in how Revly handles your data, we update this page and the date at the top. For big changes, we will also let you know in the app.
